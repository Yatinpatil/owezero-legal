Privacy Policy

OweZero · Last updated 10 October 2026

OweZero is an app for splitting shared expenses with friends, flatmates and travel groups. This policy explains what information the OweZero Android app and its sync server handle, why, and the choices you have. "We" means the developer of OweZero.

The short version: your groups and expenses stay on your phone unless you share a group. A shared group is stored on our server so that everyone with its invite link can see and edit it. You don't need an account. We don't show ads, we don't use advertising or analytics trackers, and we don't sell your information.

Information that stays on your phone

  • Your profile: your name, an optional UPI ID and your default currency.
  • Your groups: the people in each group (their names and, if you add them, phone numbers and UPI IDs), expenses (description, amount, currency, date, category, notes, itemised bills and how each one is split) and payments between people.
  • Receipt photos: when you scan a bill, the photo is read on your phone using Google ML Kit text recognition. The photo is never uploaded, is kept only in the app's temporary cache, and isn't attached to the expense.
  • Contacts: when you add someone from your contacts, Android's contact picker gives the app only the contact you choose (their name and phone number). OweZero can't read your address book.

If Android backup is turned on, this data is included in your phone's backup to your Google account, which Google encrypts and manages. CSV and PDF exports are created on your phone and only go where you send them.

Information in shared groups

When you tap Invite people in a group, the app uploads that group to the OweZero server: its name, icon and currency; each person's name, plus their phone number and UPI ID if you added them; and all of its expenses and payments. After that, changes sync between everyone in the group. Each change also carries a random identifier that the app creates when it's installed. It isn't linked to your name, phone number or Google account, and is used only to tell devices apart in the group's history.

Anyone who has a group's invite link can see the whole group and add, edit or delete its expenses, in the app or in a web browser, without an account. Treat the link like a key and share it only with the people in the group.

The server stores only a one-way hash of each invite link's secret, and all traffic to it uses HTTPS. Group contents aren't end-to-end encrypted: the server needs to read them to show the group in a web browser, so the people who run the server could technically access them. We only do that when it's needed to keep the service running or to answer a request from you.

Information other services receive

  • Exchange rates: to convert between currencies, the app downloads daily rates from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com). The request contains nothing about you or your groups, although like any website that service can see your IP address.
  • Google ML Kit: text recognition runs entirely on your phone. ML Kit sends Google diagnostic information such as your device model, app version, a per-installation identifier and performance metrics, to keep the feature working and improve it. It doesn't include your photos or the text in them. Google explains what it collects at developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure.
  • UPI apps: "Pay with UPI" opens a UPI app you choose, such as Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm or BHIM, with the payee's UPI ID and name, the amount and a note filled in. The payment happens in that app, and OweZero never sees your bank details or UPI PIN. Some UPI apps report whether the payment succeeded; OweZero uses that only to record the payment in your group.
  • Sharing: reminders, invite links and exports go through Android's share sheet to the app you pick, and that app's own privacy policy applies.

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with anyone else unless the law requires us to.

Information the server records

The server uses your IP address briefly, in memory, to limit how many requests one address can make. It doesn't keep access logs with IP addresses or invite links: its logs record only the time, type and result of each request, and old entries are deleted automatically as the logs fill up.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

  • On your phone: until you delete it. Deleting a group, expense or payment removes it straight away, and uninstalling the app removes everything.
  • On the server: a shared group is kept until a member deletes it. To do that, open the group, tap the pencil button (Edit group), then Delete for everyone. The group and everything in it are deleted from the server immediately, and from server backups within 14 days. The server keeps only a note that the link was deleted, so other members' phones can tell them. Everyone keeps the copy on their own phone. Leave group only removes the group from your phone.
  • Deleting one expense or payment in a shared group removes its details from the server. A record that it was deleted is kept so that other phones can catch up.
  • You can also email us a group's invite link and ask us to delete that group.

Children

OweZero is meant for adults and isn't directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and change the date at the top. We'll mention significant changes in the app's release notes.
